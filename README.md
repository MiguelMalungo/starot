# STAROT · Observatory of Fates

An interactive tarot reading rendered entirely in stardust. A WebGL engine holds
52,000 twinkling stars that slowly rotate like a living universe; when a spread is
dealt, they assemble into the constellation-sigil of each Major Arcana card.

## Reading it

- **A reading for myself** — deals three cards: past, present, future.
- **For someone else** — pauses for a moment of silent contemplation before the deal.
- Each card's figure is drawn from thousands of stars. Move your cursor through it
  and the tiny lens carves and swirls the swarm; lift your hand and it settles back.
- Touch **Ask again** to redeal, or **Leave an offering** to support the observatory.

## Tech

A single self-contained `index.html` — WebGL point cloud, hand-drawn arcana geometry,
no build step and no dependencies beyond Google Fonts. Just open the file, or visit
the GitHub Pages deployment.
