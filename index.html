Free tarot, star maps, numerology and tattoo flash

STAROT is a free web observatory by Porto visual artist Miguel Ferraz Guedes. Deal a three-card tarot reading, cast your birth star map, read the numbers in your name, find your Maya day sign, and turn the result into nine original tattoo flash designs with print-ready sheets in SVG and PNG.

Can I get a free tarot reading online here?

Yes. STAROT deals three cards from the 22 major arcana (past, present and future) and reads them for you, free, with no sign-up.

What is a birth star map?

It is your astral map: the sky as it stood when and where you were born, with the planets, the houses and your rising sign, computed from your birth date, time and place.

How does the numerology reading work?

It reads the numbers hidden in your name and your birth date, such as your life path, and explains what each one means.

What is my Maya day sign?

In the sacred Maya count of 260 days, every day has a sign and a tone. STAROT finds yours from your birth date, along with its cross.

What is a tattoo flash generator?

Tattoo flash is a sheet of ready-to-tattoo designs. STAROT turns your readings (your wheel, Sun and rising signs, numbers and Maya signs) into nine original designs on two flash sheets.

Is it free?

Yes. All readings are free, and the tattoo flash files are free until 15 January 2027.

Can my tattoo artist use the files?

Yes. Download the print-ready flash sheet, single designs as SVG or PNG, or everything as a ZIP, and bring them to your artist.

Is my birth data stored?

No. Your name and birth details are processed in your browser; the readings send nothing to any server, and places come from a list built into the page. Only a paid purchase would send your birth details to the payment provider; see the Terms and privacy page.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. STAROT runs in any web browser, on phone or computer, with no account and no download.

Is STAROT in Portuguese?

Yes. The whole site is available in English and in Brazilian Portuguese at starot.one/pt/.
