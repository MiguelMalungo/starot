A reading in stardust

The cards are
constellations.

Ask your question in silence. Three arcana will be drawn from the dark — past, present, future — each figure assembled from thousands of stars that scatter at your touch, and remember.

Before the cards are drawn

Hold them silently in your mind — their face, the weight of their question. Let the noise settle. The stars can wait; they have practice.

When they are clearly before you, deal.
